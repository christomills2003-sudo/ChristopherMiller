Process

A website should be planned before it is built. This process keeps the project focused on what the business actually needs.

Review

Start by reviewing the business from the customer’s point of view.

  • Business structure
  • Current online presence
  • Services or products offered
  • Consumer base and service area
  • Competitor presentation
  • Main customer action needed

Plan

Outline the page sections, wording, visual direction, calls-to-action, and mobile compatibility.

Build

Create a clean, responsive website with accessible navigation and practical contact paths.

Review & Launch

Test the page, adjust the layout, review the finished direction with the customer, make professional refinements, and prepare it for launch.

FAQ

Common questions about timelines, hosting, ownership, and what happens after a site is built.

How long does a starter website usually take?

A simple starter website can often be completed in one to three weeks, depending on how quickly content, images, business details, and feedback are provided.

Who owns the website after it is built?

After final payment, the client owns the completed website files, business content, and domain connected to the project. Miller Digital Works may retain reusable code patterns, design methods, and internal workflow materials used to build the site. Ongoing updates, hosting help, and maintenance can be handled through an optional monthly support plan.

Can hosting be handled for me?

Basic static sites can be launched through simple hosting options such as GitHub Pages or other website hosts. More advanced sites may require paid hosting, domains, forms, or backend services.

What do I need to provide before a project starts?

Helpful materials include your business name, industry, service list, preferred colors, logo or photos if available, contact information, and the main action you want customers to take.

Can the website be updated later?

Yes. The site can be updated later with new services, photos, examples, testimonials, pricing changes, or additional pages as the business grows.
